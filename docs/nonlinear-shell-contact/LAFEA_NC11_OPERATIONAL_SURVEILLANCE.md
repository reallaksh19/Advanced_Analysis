# LAFEA-NC NC-11 — Operational Surveillance and Requalification

NC-11 governs continued operation after NC-10 run receipts exist. A previously authorized deployment cannot retain authority merely because its initial release gate passed.

## Pinned scope

The phase binds telemetry, alert thresholds, incident records, replay samples, authorization expiry, revocation, requalification decisions, and periodic reviews to exact governed run receipts. Build, configuration, schema, custody, and result drift are fail-closed.

## Suspension rule

A critical incident, audit gap, unreviewed drift, false-negative monitoring result, custody break, expired authorization, or failed control test suspends operational authority. Reinstatement is never automatic and requires a new governed receipt after human-approved requalification.

## Authority boundary

NC-11 does not authorize autonomous case disposition, automatic asset acceptance, fitness-for-service, remaining strength, or merging. Contract CI qualifies only the surveillance schema and evaluator; production authority remains dependent on complete external evidence and current human authorization.
