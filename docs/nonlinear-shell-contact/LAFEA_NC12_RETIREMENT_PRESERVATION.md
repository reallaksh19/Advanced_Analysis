# LAFEA-NC NC-12 — Retirement and Evidence Preservation

NC-12 governs the final withdrawal of an NC-11 monitored execution capability. Retirement is not a temporary suspension and does not create a dormant production reactivation path.

## Pinned scope

The phase requires owner-approved irreversible authorization withdrawal, removal of execution, release, and administrative privileges, deployment teardown, exact source/build/configuration archival, complete receipt-chain and case/audit ledger preservation, open-case transfer, successor mapping, retention and privacy controls, and a signed closeout report.

## Verification

At least two independent verifiers must demonstrate that no active credentials, deployments, orphaned cases, archive-integrity failures, or executable production paths remain. Read-only archive recovery must reproduce retained evidence without restoring production capability.

## Authority boundary

NC-12 cannot authorize production reactivation, production execution, autonomous case disposition, automatic asset acceptance, fitness-for-service, remaining strength, or merging. Contract CI qualifies only the retirement schema and evaluator; actual closeout requires complete external evidence.
