# M022-A review guide

Review this PR as a contract and evidence boundary only.

The load-bearing review questions are:

1. Do the status and provenance rules prevent silent StagedJSON carry-forward?
2. Are reference, operating, and design temperatures represented independently?
3. Does the resolved-analysis record preserve missing hot material states rather than accepting cold stiffness?
4. Does the real fixture check prove the selected-branch values and M008-C limitations directly?
5. Are canonical projection, solver wiring, UI, and InputXML excluded?
